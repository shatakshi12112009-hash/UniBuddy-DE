Student Guide: Germany 🇩🇪

A practical guide to studying, living, and building a future in Germany.

🎓 1. University & Admissions

* Research universities, courses, fees, and entry requirements.
* Prepare certificates, transcripts, and language test results.
* Track application deadlines and scholarship opportunities.
* Check requirements directly with your chosen university.

Resources: DAAD · Study in Germany · uni-assist

🛂 2. Visa & Documents

* Check current visa and residence requirements.
* Keep important documents and secure digital copies.
* Follow official guidance for your nationality and situation.

Resource: German Missions in India

🏠 3. Accommodation & Daily Life

* Explore student residences and shared apartments (WGs).
* Check rent, deposits, contracts, and additional charges.
* Learn local transport, registration, and university procedures.
* Verify accommodation before sending money.

💶 4. Money & Healthcare

* Budget for rent, food, transport, insurance, and study materials.
* Track expenses and plan emergency savings.
* Confirm health insurance requirements before enrolment.

📚 5. Study Smarter

* Review lectures regularly and practise with exercises.
* Track assignments and examination deadlines.
* Understand examination rules and academic integrity.
* Use tutorials, study groups, and university support services.

💻 6. Technology & Skills

Useful skills include programming, databases, problem-solving, Git, and GitHub.

Free learning resources:

* CS50
* freeCodeCamp
* GitHub Skills
* Python Documentation

Build small projects and document them clearly in your GitHub repositories.

🇩🇪 7. Learn German

Practise vocabulary, listening, speaking, and writing regularly.

Useful phrases:

* Guten Tag — Hello
* Ich habe eine Frage. — I have a question.
* Können Sie mir helfen? — Can you help me?
* Vielen Dank. — Thank you very much.

Resource: Learn German — Deutsche Welle

💼 8. Career & Internships

* Prepare a clear CV and professional portfolio.
* Develop communication and teamwork skills.
* Explore internships and university career services.
* Check applicable work permissions before accepting a job.

Resource: Make it in Germany

🧠 9. Well-Being

* Maintain a balanced study routine.
* Make time for rest, exercise, and social connections.
* Contact university counselling or student support when needed.
